Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

AlwaysOpen AI builds AI Lead & Booking Agents for small businesses. This page explains, in simple words, what information we collect, how we use it, and how you can ask us to delete it.

1. What we collect

When you contact us for a demo, or when you message a business that uses our AI agent, we may collect:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number (for WhatsApp, Telegram, Instagram or Messenger)
  • Your business name and what your business does
  • The booking details you give us — service, date and time
  • Your email address, only if you send us an email or fill in a form

We do not ask for passwords, card details, or government ID numbers.

2. How we use it

We use this information only to:

  • Reply to your messages
  • Take and confirm your booking
  • Answer your questions about a demo or a service

We do not sell your information. We do not share it with advertisers.

3. Who sees it

Your information is seen by AlwaysOpen AI and by the small business you contacted. It may also pass through the tools we use to run the agent — for example WhatsApp, n8n, and Google Sheets — only so the agent can reply and save your booking.

4. How long we keep it

We keep booking information only for as long as the business needs it to serve you. You can ask us to delete it at any time — see section 5.

5. How to delete your data

If you want us to delete your information, send an email to hello.alwaysopenai@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my data”, and tell us your name and phone number. We will delete the information we hold about you and confirm by email.

6. Contact us

If you have any question about this policy, email us at hello.alwaysopenai@gmail.com.
